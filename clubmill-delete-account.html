Delete your account

Last updated: November 14, 2026

You can delete your ClubMill account from inside the game. If you no longer have ClubMill installed, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete in the app

  1. Open ClubMill.
  2. Open Options.
  3. Choose delete account.
  4. Confirm.

You can play ClubMill without an account. These steps apply when you have signed in and a player account exists.

What we delete

When the deletion succeeds, we remove:

  • Your Azure PlayFab player account
  • Sign-ins linked to that account, such as Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google
  • Leaderboard entries for that account, including the display name stored on the server

What stays on the device

Until you uninstall ClubMill or clear its data, the device can still hold:

  • Virtual coins stored on that device
  • Game progress stored on that device

Deleting the account does not uninstall the app. It also does not close your Apple ID, Google Account, or Steam account. Those companies keep their own records under their own policies.

If you no longer have the app

Email support@purpletee.ai with the subject line "ClubMill account deletion".

Include:

  • The platform you used to sign in: Apple, Google, or Steam
  • Your leaderboard display name
  • A player ID, if the game has shown you one

We tell PlayFab not to store your Google email address. An email address by itself may not be enough to find an Android account. The display name, the platform, and any player ID help us match the PlayFab account.

Mail you have already sent us may remain in our inbox as ordinary correspondence about the request.

How long it takes

If you delete the account in the game and the request succeeds, we remove the PlayFab account, the linked sign-ins, and the leaderboard entries as part of that request. If the game tells you the deletion did not succeed, email support@purpletee.ai.

If you email a deletion request, we aim to complete it within 30 days of receiving enough information to find the account.
