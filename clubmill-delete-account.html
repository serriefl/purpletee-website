Delete your account

Last updated: November 14, 2026

You delete a Clubhouse Millionaire (ClubMill) account in the game, after you sign in with the same Apple account, or the same Google account once that sign-in exists. That works after you reinstall the app and on a new device. We do not delete an account because of an email. We cannot verify that an email is from the account holder, so we will not delete an account on request.

On iPhone

  1. Open Clubhouse Millionaire. If you removed the app, install it again. A new iPhone works the same way.
  2. Open the Leaderboard screen.
  3. Sign in with the same Apple account (Sign in with Apple).
  4. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

You can play without an account. These steps are for an account you created by signing in.

On Android

Sign in with Google is not in the game today. It is coming soon. When it is offered, deletion will work the same way:

  1. Open Clubhouse Millionaire. A reinstall or a new device is fine.
  2. Open the Leaderboard screen.
  3. Sign in with the same Google account.
  4. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Until that sign-in exists, there is no Google-linked account to delete.

On Steam

A Steam sign-in is not offered. There is no Steam account to delete. If a PC sign-in is added later, deletion will still be in the game after you sign in with that same account, not by email.

What deletion removes

When Delete Account succeeds, we remove:

  • The Azure PlayFab account
  • The sign-in linked to that account
  • On iPhone, we also send Apple a request to revoke the refresh token we stored for that sign-in. Apple's reply does not always confirm the revoke

What is kept, and for how long

On the device, coins and game progress stay until you remove the app. Deleting the account does not remove them and does not uninstall the app. It also does not close your Apple ID or Google Account. Those companies keep their own records under their own policies.

Leaderboard rows are not part of that deletion. The game does not run a separate delete of those rows, and a row has been seen to come back after an account was deleted. A display name, a score, or a highest balance on a public board can remain. We do not know a date when a remaining row will disappear.

PlayFab keeps a login history with the account: IP address, device information, and an approximate location taken from the IP. That history lasts about 30 days. We do not keep a separate copy of it. When deletion of the PlayFab account succeeds, that account record is what the deletion removes, including login history still on it. We have not checked every PlayFab log after a deletion.

Email you send us can stay in our mail as ordinary correspondence. It is not a deletion request we will carry out.

Email is for help, not deletion

If you cannot find Delete Account, email support@purpletee.ai. We can explain the steps above. We will not delete the account from that message.
