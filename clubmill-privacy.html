ClubMill

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Purple Tee LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates ClubMill, a sports arcade game for phones and, when we offer it, personal computers ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play ClubMill on iPhone, Android, or a PC version.

This policy covers ClubMill only. It does not cover Local Links or any other Purple Tee LLC app.

In short

  • You can play without an account.
  • If you sign in, Azure PlayFab keeps a player account ID, the linked sign-in, and your leaderboard display name.
  • Coins and game progress stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your information. ClubMill is not real-money gambling.

1. Information We Collect

ClubMill does not require an account, and we do not ask you to create a Purple Tee username and password.

If you choose to sign in, or if you contact us, the Game may handle:

  • A player account ID created by our game backend
  • The sign-in identity linked to that account, as described for Apple, Google, and Steam below
  • A leaderboard display name, stored on our servers and moderated by us
  • Virtual coins and game progress, stored locally on your device
  • Messages you send to support@purpletee.ai

Sign-in and leaderboard requests are sent to Microsoft Azure PlayFab over encrypted HTTPS. The sign-in sections below say what each platform shares.

2. Sign in with Apple

On iPhone, you may sign in with Sign in with Apple. This is optional. You can play without it.

Apple provides an identifier issued for ClubMill so the Game can recognize the same player when you return. We use that identifier to link your player account and your leaderboard display name.

We use Sign in with Apple to run your account. We do not use it to build an advertising profile, and we do not sell the identifier Apple shares with us.

3. Sign in with Google

On Android, ClubMill may offer Sign in with Google. That option is coming soon. You can play on Android without an account.

When Sign in with Google is offered, we request only basic identity information through OpenID, which is the information needed to sign you in. We tell Azure PlayFab not to store your Google email address.

We use the Google sign-in to link your player account. We do not use it to email you, to advertise to you, or to look through the rest of your Google account.

4. Steam

We may offer a Steam sign-in later, for a PC version of ClubMill. Steam sign-in is not available today, and ClubMill does not collect a Steam identity now.

If we offer Steam sign-in, we will use it to recognize your player account, in the same way we use the other sign-in options, and we will update this policy with the specific information that sign-in shares. Until that update, this page does not describe a Steam sign-in that is not in the Game.

5. Azure PlayFab and Leaderboards

ClubMill uses Microsoft Azure PlayFab as its game backend. When you sign in, PlayFab stores:

  • A player account ID
  • The sign-in identity linked to that account
  • Your leaderboard display name

You choose a display name in the Game. We store it on our servers, we moderate it, and other players can see that name with your score. We can change or remove a name that breaks the rules in our terms.

PlayFab processes this information for us so sign-in and leaderboards can work. We do not allow that information to be sold.

6. Coins and Progress on Your Device

Virtual coins and game progress are stored locally on your device. They are not kept as a balance in PlayFab.

If you uninstall ClubMill, clear its data, or lose the device, the coins and progress on that device can be gone. A leaderboard entry can remain on the server until you delete your account or we remove it.

Coins have no cash value. ClubMill does not pay money, and you cannot redeem or exchange coins for money or goods.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the Game: open Options, then delete account. Deletion removes your PlayFab player account, the sign-ins linked to it, and your leaderboard entries, including the display name stored on the server.

Coins and progress stored only on the device stay on the device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

If you no longer have the Game installed, you can email support@purpletee.ai and ask us to delete the account. The steps, and what we need in order to find the account, are on the delete your account page. Because we tell PlayFab not to store a Google email address, an email address by itself may not match an Android account.

8. How We Use Information

  • Run optional sign-in and keep your player account
  • Show and moderate leaderboards
  • Answer support mail and deletion requests
  • Protect the Game and enforce the rules against cheating and abusive names

We do not use ClubMill information for advertising.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We share the account information described above with Microsoft Azure PlayFab, which hosts player accounts and leaderboards for us.

Apple, Google, or Valve may process information when you use their store or their sign-in. Their own privacy policies describe that processing. We receive the sign-in identity those services share with ClubMill, as described in the sign-in sections above.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to investigate cheating, abuse, or a security problem.

We do not share ClubMill information with advertising networks, and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

10. Advertising

ClubMill does not show third-party advertisements. We do not sell personal information. We do not use information from ClubMill to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising.

If we add a tool that collects diagnostics or usage data, we will update this policy, name the tool, and describe what it collects.

11. Data Retention

We keep the PlayFab player account, the linked sign-in, and the leaderboard display name until you delete the account, or until we remove them because they break the rules. A moderated name or a cheated score may be removed without a deletion request from you.

We do not keep a server copy of the coins and progress stored on your device. That information stays on the device until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

If you email us, we keep that correspondence as an ordinary business record for as long as we need it to handle your request.

12. Your Choices

  • Play without signing in.
  • Delete an account you created, from Options in the Game.
  • Email support@purpletee.ai if you no longer have the Game and want the account deleted.
  • Uninstall ClubMill, or clear its data, to remove coins and progress stored on the device.

You can also email us with a question about this policy or about the information described above.

13. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect the information we handle. Connections between the Game and Azure PlayFab use encrypted HTTPS.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14. Children's Privacy

ClubMill is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email support@purpletee.ai and we will delete that account.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.

16. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about ClubMill privacy, contact us at:

Email: support@purpletee.ai
