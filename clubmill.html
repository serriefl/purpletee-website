The short version

Sports cabinets, arcade coins, a board if you want one.

ClubMill is a slot-style arcade from Purple Tee LLC. Play a golf, football, soccer, or baseball cabinet, collect a free coin top-up each day, and put a moderated name on the leaderboard. Sign-in is optional. On iPhone you can use Sign in with Apple. Sign in with Google on Android is coming soon, and a Steam version comes later. Coins stay on your device. They have no cash value, and ClubMill is not real-money gambling.

In the game

What you can do

  • Four cabinets

    Golf, football, soccer, and baseball. Each cabinet is its own slot-style game.

  • Daily coins

    Collect a free coin top-up each day. Coins are for play inside ClubMill and have no cash value.

  • Leaderboards

    Set a display name and post a score. Names are stored on our servers, and we moderate them.

  • Optional sign-in

    Play without an account. Sign in with Apple on iPhone. Sign in with Google on Android is coming soon. A Steam sign-in may come later.

  • On your device

    Coins and game progress are stored on the device you play on. Uninstalling the game removes that local progress.

  • Entertainment only

    ClubMill is not real-money gambling. You cannot redeem or exchange coins for money or goods.
